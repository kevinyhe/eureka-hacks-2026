Learn to Box with your Phone

Phone Orientation

Hold phone vertically upwards with screen facing left

Sensor Data

    Accelerometer

  • Uses your phone's built in accelerometer
    - Determines which direction your arm is moving
    - Decides which attack or move to choose

    Gyroscope

  • Uses your phone's built in gyroscope
    - Determines the angle at which the device is held
    - Decides whether or not attacks or moves can be initiated

Game Mechanics

  • Game Duration: 90 seconds
  • How to win:
    - Knock your opponent out!
    - Have more HP than your opponent end of the match
  • Health Bar: 100 HP
  • Damage Types:
    - Jab: 5 damage, fastest attack
    - Right Hook: 7 damage, slight stun
    - Uppercut: 10 damage, longer windup
  • Last 30 Seconds: Double damage

Stamina Mechanics

  • Each player has a stamina bar which slowly regenerates over time
  • Attacks consume different stamina:
    - Jabs: Low stamina cost
    - Right Hooks: Medium stamina
    - Uppercuts: High stamina cost
    - Block: Regenerates stamina
  • Low stamina reduces punch damage and attack speed

Advanced Mechanics

  • Combo Bar: Increases punch damage and attack speed if many shots are landed consecutively
  • Blocking: Reduces incoming damage but prevents the user from attacking during this time
  • Dodge Mechanic:
    - Tilt the phone left/right to dodge
    - If a strike is successfully dodged, you will charge up both your stamina and combo!
